# Venue notes: systems, cloud, networking and security journals

Collected on 23 October 2026. Quotes are verbatim from the pages named. Many publisher sites block automated fetching, so Elsevier (ScienceDirect), ACM and Wiley facts come from Wayback Machine snapshots dated 2024–2026. **Policies change.** Always prefer the venue's live pages opened during the run, and use this file only as a fallback, labelled with this date.

Days to first decision are the publisher's own figures where given. A median of 2–5 days usually means heavy desk screening, so the front matter has to carry the paper alone.

## IEEE

**IEEE Transactions on Parallel and Distributed Systems (TPDS)** · https://www.computer.org/digital-library/journals/td/cfp-parallel-distributed-systems
- Scope: "parallel and distributed systems research areas of current importance to our readers"; "communication and synchronization protocols, network algorithms, scheduling, and load balancing"; "resource management including green computing, middleware for grids, clouds, and data centers".
- Extensions of conference papers: "Allowable but insufficient" additions include "Extension to background and/or related work" and "Additional experimental results without new insights". Unacceptable: "Simple union of content from multiple prior publications".
- Length: regular paper 12 double-column pages, up to 18 with over-length charges; survey 14; comments 2.

**IEEE Transactions on Cloud Computing (TCC)** · https://www.computer.org/digital-library/journals/cc/cfp-ieee-transactions-cloud-computing
- Scope: "dedicated to the multidisciplinary field of cloud computing"; "resource management and optimization in the cloud"; "cloud security and privacy"; "cloud computing regulations and compliance".
- Desk triggers: "Survey papers are not accepted into peer review for TCC." Extensions need "at least 35%" new material, a cover letter, a new title and a citation of the earlier paper, otherwise "'desk' rejected without any reviews."
- Length: regular 12 pages, up to 18 with over-length charges.

**IEEE Transactions on Dependable and Secure Computing (TDSC)** · https://www.computer.org/digital-library/journals/tq/cfp-dependable-secure-computing
- Scope: "foundations, methodologies, and mechanisms that support the achievement–through design, modeling, and evaluation–of systems and networks that are dependable and secure".
- Out of scope: "purely theoretical interest in the area of cryptography without a clear and manifest application"; "generic systems-management problems, as opposed to specific dependability and security challenges"; "device-level, as opposed to system-level" work.
- Extensions: "expanded motivation, expanded discussion of related work, variants of previously reported algorithms, incremental additional experiments/simulations… will fall below the line for proceeding with review."
- Policies: at most two submissions per author per month; resubmission of a rejected paper only after 6 months.
- Length: regular 12 pages, up to 18 with over-length charges; short 8.

**IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security (TIFS)** · https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/ieee-transactions-information-forensics-and-security
- Scope: "covers the sciences, technologies, and applications relating to information forensics, information security, biometrics, surveillance and systems applications that incorporate these features."
- Signal Processing Society rules: a paper is "a candidate for an Immediate Rejection if it lacks novelty, e.g., a straightforward combination of well-established, repeatable theories and algorithms within a known field of interest"; also rejected without review for insufficient experimental data, poor English or too few citations.
- Policies: list all posted preprints with URLs at submission; "unacceptable for any section of a manuscript to be entirely produced using an LLM".
- Length: 13 double-column pages at first submission, 16 at revision.

**IEEE Transactions on Services Computing (TSC)** · https://www.computer.org/digital-library/journals/sc/cfp-services-computing
- Scope: "all computing and software aspects of the science and technology of services innovation"; topic area "AI and Service Computing: the application of AI for service computing and the application of service computing in AI systems".
- Limits: "only service-oriented grid computing topics will be covered"; resubmissions within 60 days of rejection are not considered.
- Length: regular 12 pages (14 after major revision); survey-tutorial 18; brief contribution 8.

**IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM)** · https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-tnsm
- Scope: "timely publication of archival research on the management of networks, systems, services and applications"; papers "should contain significant contributions in the field of Network and Service Management".
- Policies: resubmitting a rejected paper is not allowed; extensions need a different title and a list of extensions in the cover letter.
- Length: free up to 10 pages; over-length charges up to 16.

**IEEE Internet of Things Journal** · https://ieee-iotj.org/guidelines-for-authors/
- Scope: "articles on the latest advances, as well as review articles, on the various aspects of IoT"; "service-oriented IoT architecture"; "IoT service middleware".
- Policies: duplicate or unextended resubmissions are "immediately rejected", and the authors may be barred "for at least one calendar year".
- Length: over-length charges beyond eight pages; abstract 150–250 words.

**All IEEE journals:** AI-generated content "shall be disclosed in the acknowledgments section", naming the system, the sections and the level of use; editing-only use is recommended, not required, for disclosure (https://open.ieee.org/author-guidelines-for-artificial-intelligence-ai-generated-text/).

## ACM

**ACM Transactions on Internet Technology (TOIT)** (snapshots February and June 2025)
- Scope: "design, use, analysis, engineering, and governance of Internet, Internet scale computing systems, applications, and services"; topic list includes "Compliance", "Laws and regulations", "Cloud computing and edge computing".
- Desk-rejection grounds: "simultaneous double submission… the subject of the paper being outside the coverage scope of TOIT, the paper obviously lacking in breadth, substance, quality or completeness."
- Policies: disclose overlap in the cover letter ("A failure by authors to disclose their prior work is grounds for rejection"); no resubmission for 12 months after a rejection.
- Length: "Recommended page length for submissions is 20-25 pages. Submissions should be no longer than 30 pages."

**ACM Transactions on Privacy and Security (TOPS)** (snapshots July and August 2025)
- Scope: "devoted to the study, analysis, and application of information security and privacy"; "practical relevance to the construction, evaluation, application, or operation of security or privacy-critical systems".
- Excluded: "Tutorial or survey papers are not suitable for TOPS."; "Submissions on watermarking or steganography are currently out of scope"; "Minor enhancements to conference papers will not be accepted."
- Length: "Submissions must not exceed 35 pages TOTAL in the ACM style." "Papers over 35 pages will be rejected without review."

**ACM Computing Surveys** (snapshots 2025–2026)
- Scope: "summarizes and organizes recent research results in a novel way that integrates and adds understanding".
- Excluded: "Computing Surveys does not publish "new" research."; "Manuscripts of excessive length may be rejected without review."
- Length: long survey 35 pages including references; short survey 15.

**All ACM journals** (authorship policy, May 2026 snapshot): AI used in the research must be described in the methods section; check the live policy for writing-only use.

## Elsevier and KeAi

**Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing (JPDC)**
- Scope: "original research papers and timely review articles on the theory, design, evaluation, and use of parallel and/or distributed computing systems"; "Cloud, edge and fog computing".
- Length: at most 35 double-spaced pages, excluding title, abstract and references; Research Notes 15. About 25 days to first decision.

**Future Generation Computer Systems (FGCS)** · https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/future-generation-computer-systems/0167-739X
- Scope: "Distributed dynamic resource management and scheduling"; "Security aspects"; "Big Data on such infrastructures as grids, clouds and the Internet of Things".
- About 3 days to first decision.

**Journal of Network and Computer Applications (JNCA)** · https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-network-and-computer-applications/1084-8045
- Scope: "all areas relating to computer networks and applications thereof"; "computing models such as cloud computing"; "applications of security in computer and networks".
- Pre-review screen "for sufficient contribution, relevance to the scope of the journal, timeliness of the topic, organization and writing"; if a paper fails, "a detailed reviewer comment is not available." About 11 days to first decision.

**Computer Networks**
- Scope: "complete coverage of all topics of interest to those involved in the computer communications networking area"; "Network Security and Privacy"; "Network Operation and Management".
- Duplicate or simultaneous submissions are "immediately rejected". About 5 days to first decision.

**Computers & Security** · https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/computers-and-security/0167-4048
- Scope: "computer security, audit, control and data integrity".
- Excluded: cryptology, "including blockchains, watermarking, and steganography"; "As of early 2024, we have instituted a moratorium on consideration of submissions that feature AI or ML as significant components… items directed to the security of AI/ML systems themselves (such as LLM and federated learning) are out of scope".
- About 2 days to first decision.

**Journal of Information Security and Applications (JISA)**
- Scope: "original research and practice-driven applications with relevance to information security".
- Not considered: "routine application of existing AI/ML models to standard security datasets"; "Pure mathematical proofs without a demonstrated industrial and practical application"; hardware-security work "based solely on simulations"; "Purely descriptive reviews". About 3 days to first decision.

**Journal of Systems and Software (JSS)** · https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-systems-and-software/0164-1212
- Scope: "All articles should provide evidence to support their claims, e.g. through empirical studies, simulation, formal proofs"; contributions should hold "independently of specific application domains".
- Length: under 18 double-column pages encouraged. Acceptance rate 15.0%. About 5 days to first decision.

**Applied Soft Computing**
- Scope: "an integrated view of soft computing to solve real life problems"; "Fuzzy Logic, Neural Networks, Evolutionary Computing, Swarm Intelligence". Papers without soft-computing methods are at scope risk.
- Length: technical papers 20–30 pages, at most 50.

**Digital Communications and Networks (KeAi)** · https://www.keaipublishing.com/en/journals/digital-communications-and-networks/
- Scope: "communication systems and networks"; "Edge, Fog, and Cloud Computing and Networks"; "Trust, Security, and Privacy Protocols".
- Desk triggers: "desk rejected for reasons like out of scope, ethical conflicts, high similarities"; "lack of interest to the readership".

**Array**
- Scope: broad computer science. "Submissions must be novel, technically sound, and clearly presented." About 9 days to first decision.

**All Elsevier journals:** a standalone "Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process" immediately before the references (https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals).

## Springer

**Cluster Computing** · https://link.springer.com/journal/10586/aims-and-scope
- Scope: "unify the fields of parallel processing, distributed computing systems and high performance computer networks"; "high performance distributed computing systems that include Clouds, Grids, and Data Centers".
- Desk triggers: "A concern raised by a single peer reviewer or the Editor themself may result in the manuscript being rejected"; incomplete editable source files mean the article is "not being considered for review". A Data Availability Statement is required. About 25 days median to first decision.

**Journal of Cloud Computing** · https://link.springer.com/journal/13677/aims-and-scope
- Scope: "research articles on all aspects of Cloud Computing"; "Thorough examination of Clouds with respect to issues of management, governance, trust and privacy, and interoperability, are also in scope."
- Policies: fully open access; LLM use documented in the Methods section; the cover letter must give "An explanation of why your manuscript should be published in Journal of Cloud Computing".

## Wiley

**Software: Practice and Experience** (snapshots 2023–2025)
- Scope: "practical experience with new and established techniques and tool for both software systems and applications"; "Distributed Computing Paradigms: Service, Cloud, Fog, Edge."
- Desk triggers: "A large fraction of new submissions to the journal are rejected without being refereed because the editors consider the topics… outside the journal's scope." Software process, methodology, project management, requirements and verification and validation are out of scope.
- Length: regular papers at most 40 pages. Public data archiving is a condition for publication. Acceptance rate 12%.
